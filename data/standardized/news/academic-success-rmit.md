# Student Academic Success (SAS)

Student Academic Success provides learning support for current RMIT students. The service describes support for planning and receiving feedback on assignments, using AI to study more effectively, improving understanding of course content, and developing skills and strategies that help students achieve their study objectives.

Students can explore the Writing and Learning Centre and Peer-Assisted Learning services. The Writing and Learning Centre provides individual or group support from a Learning Advisor on assignments, and Learning Advisors also deliver workshops for courses or programs. Peer-Assisted Learning can help students learn collaboratively and practise course-related skills.

Students can drop in at service locations, book one-to-one support online, and check the workshop schedule. The RMIT Learning Lab is described as a collection of learning resources. This snapshot is a concise record of the official page; students should use the source URL for current locations, booking options, workshop times and eligibility.
