# Scholarships

RMIT University Vietnam provides a range of scholarships to promising students each year. The official scholarship page is an overview that directs future students to undergraduate and postgraduate scholarship opportunities and explains that applicants should explore the available options and apply through the relevant process. It describes scholarships as support for students who demonstrate characteristics and achievements valued by RMIT.

The page contains separate pathways for future students and links to information about scholarship categories, eligibility and applications. A scholarship opportunity is not automatically available to every applicant: the page says that the application process is highly competitive and that the number of scholarships is limited. Applicants should therefore check the conditions, supporting documents, intake and deadline on the specific scholarship page before relying on an award.

This snapshot is a source record for the RAG corpus. It does not replace the official scholarship terms, which may change between intakes. Use the URL above to verify the current scholarship categories, eligibility requirements and application instructions.
