# Book a study room

RMIT Library offers bookable study rooms for learning, collaboration and assignment preparation. Students can check availability for the Beanland and Hanoi libraries, then log in with their RMIT account, choose a campus, select a room and time, and confirm the booking. The page links to the study-room booking instructions and describes the booking policy.

The policy says that rooms can be booked up to two weeks in advance, with a maximum of one hour per slot and two bookings per person. A representative of a student group must check in with a librarian at the library front desk using an RMIT ID. Students should check in within the stated time before their booking; otherwise a booking may be cancelled and made available to others. The booking system only allows reservations during Library operating hours.

Users must clean up after using the meeting room. Available equipment includes a whiteboard, TV screen, table and chairs, with an eraser and markers available on request. Check the official page for current availability and rules.
