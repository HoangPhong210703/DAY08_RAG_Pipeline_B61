# Tuition fees

RMIT Vietnam publishes tuition fees and related charges for students who are new to the University and enrolled in a Higher Education program. Tuition fee payments are made each semester on a course-by-course basis, so the amount depends on the courses in a student's enrolment rather than being one identical payment for every student. The page explains that the displayed annual tuition fee is indicative and based on an average study load.

Students should read the relevant Student Fees & Charges Guide and contact RMIT if they are unsure how a fee applies to their enrolment. The page also separates English for University and UniSTART Academic fees from higher-education program fees, and links to application and support information. It notes that medical insurance arrangements and fees can apply to international students, while the National Medical Insurance Fee is paid by Vietnamese university students. Fees may change, and the page identifies the applicable year and terms and conditions.

This landing snapshot preserves the source URL for verification; the official page remains the authority for current amounts, deadlines and individual payment instructions.
